About S&S Legal

A focused firm, founded in 2017.

Established in 2017 by Richmond Numbo Saaka, S&S Legal provides a range of corporate services, and resolves disputes through litigation in court and alternative dispute resolution such as arbitration.

Why clients choose us

Our lawyers built their practice at some of Ghana’s notable firms. Work is done by a small, focused team that concentrates on delivering excellent legal service to each client, without bureaucracy or layers of redundancy.

Experience that counts

Legal practice and expertise gained at established firms in Accra and Kumasi, and in university teaching and research.

Faster turnaround

No layers of bureaucracy. Work moves quickly from instruction to advice, filing or signature.

Full attention to detail

Your matter is handled by lawyers who know it well and are never too busy to answer.

Working with us
