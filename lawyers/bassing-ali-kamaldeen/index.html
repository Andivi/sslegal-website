Profile

Bassing Ali Kamaldeen is undertaking his pupillage at S&S Legal and pursuing a Master of Laws in Energy Law at the University of Ghana, Legon.

His research focuses on the upstream and downstream energy sectors, including petroleum, oil and gas, and electricity markets. He brings an interdisciplinary foundation in law, economic policy management and the social sciences.

Before joining S&S Legal he gained experience with the Legal Aid Commission and the Parliament of Ghana, working on public law, advocacy and legislative processes.
