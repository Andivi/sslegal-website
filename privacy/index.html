What we collect

When you request a consultation, email or call us, we receive the details you choose to share: your name, phone number, email address and a description of your matter.

We do not sell your information. We share it only where needed to provide legal services to you, or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Write to info@sslegal.org.
